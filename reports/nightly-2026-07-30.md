# Nightly Folly — 2026-07-30

Previous known-good commit: `a676932a2177de10cb1c9f0740160cd81dd29384`.

## Selection and hypothesis

The current Today experience had stronger potential than a new shallow Folly, so
this run improved **The Constellation That Waited for You**. Two scoped
directions were considered: teach the constellation a two-beat rhythm, or let
each star arrive as Glow, Drift, or Dare. The latter was selected because choice
could affect every step and the ending.

Fun hypothesis: choosing each star's character makes the evolving sky feel
authored and its final name worth replaying for.

## Version 1 playtest

The original was immediately clear and visually calm, but six identical clicks
always produced the same sky. The premise promised autonomy while the player had
none; the ending was a sentence rather than a payoff.

surprise 2.5, progression 3.5, emotional payoff 2.5, replayability 1.0, visual

## Product iteration 1

Added a choice before every placement: Glow, Drift, or Dare. Each choice changes
the star symbol, color, connecting line, and the sky's emerging temperament.
After six choices, the complete sequence resolves deterministically into a named
constellation such as **The Steadfast Lantern**, **The Patient Tide**, or an
alternate mixed-path result. “Try another sky” makes replay intent explicit.

Round 2/final scores: clarity 4.5, responsiveness 4.5, agency 4.0, surprise
4.0, progression 4.5, emotional payoff 4.0, replayability 4.0, visual coherence
4.5, mobile usability 4.5, memorability 4.0. Average: 4.25.

The final loop is choose → place → watch the pattern develop → discover its name
→ replay with a different sequence.
